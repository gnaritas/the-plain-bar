Privacy · The Plain Bar

What this site knows about you.

Short version: only what you type into the contact form, plus whatever Google's map embed does on its own. No analytics, no tracking scripts, no cookies set by this site.

The contact form

If you use the form, you send the bar your name, an optional phone number, an optional email address, and your message. That information is used for one thing: answering you. Messages are emailed to the bar's inbox and kept as long as needed to reply and to remember what was asked.

No trackers

This site loads no analytics, no advertising scripts and no third-party cookies of its own, and it sets no cookies itself. The only outside content on the site is the embedded Google Map on the home and contact pages, which loads from Google — Google may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy once the map renders.

Your mail comes from the bar

Replies go out as demarkus@acktl.com. If you'd rather not be emailed about the bar at all, say so in any reply or email the owner and the bar will stop.

Removing your details

Email demarkus@acktl.com and ask — the bar will delete what the form kept about you.

This page describes the site as it stands today, November 2026, before opening. If practices change at launch, this page changes with them.

Anything else?

One question, one sentence — the form works.
