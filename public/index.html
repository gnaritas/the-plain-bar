A plain name for a bar built around getting together.

02 · The story so far

Renovation is already underway.

Owner DeMarkus King is turning a 900-square-foot space inside Longview Mall into the kind of bar Longview can walk into without a plan — and filling the calendar before the opening: brunch on Sundays, book nights, and classes for people who want to get better at making a drink. The team is just forming; the bar is hiring its first bar manager now.
